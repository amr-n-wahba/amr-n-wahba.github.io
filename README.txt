How to put this site online (free)
1. Netlify: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder. You get a link.
2. Or GitHub Pages: create a repo, upload these files, Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch (main, root).
Contact form: the first message sent from the live site triggers an activation email from FormSubmit to your inbox. Click Activate once; after that every message arrives in your email.
Edit your data (links, projects, certificates) in the SITE block at the top of the script inside index.html.
